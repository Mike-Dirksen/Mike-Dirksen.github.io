About

I grew up in Lincoln, Nebraska, with questions that often felt too large for small-town Nebraska. I love the theory of games and watching science fiction because, much like mathematics, they introduce us to concepts that exceed the scenarios possible within our own lives.

The University of Chicago—and the peers I've met along the way—has largely been an extension of that exploration: a place to keep asking bigger questions and find people eager to ask them with me.
