The monkey, the keyboard, and “abracadabra”

How long should we expect to wait for random typing to produce one particular word—and why do repeated patterns make the answer more interesting?

August 19, 2026 · Draft

Working template. This note is intentionally skeletal. Examples, derivations, and Michael's preferred framing will be added later.

The setup

Imagine a monkey pressing keys independently and uniformly at random. We wait until the most recent letters spell abracadabra. What should the average waiting time be?

The tempting shortcut

If the alphabet has k possible keys and the target has eleven letters, a first guess is built from the probability of producing the full word in one eleven-letter block. That guess is useful—but it misses the way candidate words can overlap.

Why overlap matters

The ending of abracadabra partially resembles its beginning. A failed attempt can therefore leave useful progress toward the next attempt instead of resetting the process completely.
